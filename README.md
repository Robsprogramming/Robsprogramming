### How I build

1. **Private by default.** Your data stays on your device. Where an app talks to AI, it uses your own key and shows you exactly what would be sent, first.
2. **Honest metrics.** No number is displayed that the app didn't actually measure. A score you can game is a score that lies to you.
3. **Built to be trusted.** The unglamorous reliability work, the retries and rate limits and repair layers, is the part that makes small software worth paying for.
